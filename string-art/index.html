    How it works

    Nail 0 is at the middle of the right side; numbers increase counter-clockwise. From the current nail, the program takes the line that crosses the most area still needing thread, then lowers that need under it. With one thread, it stops when no line brings the image closer to the photo. Each nail pair is used once per thread.

    With several threads, each area gets the thread mix over the board color closest to the photo, and lines are shared by how much each thread covers. A thread with under 3% of the lines is removed.

    Find best settings makes small test versions and compares each with the photo, both blurred as seen from a distance: color difference, light and dark patterns, fine detail and color patterns. It tries preset palettes and DMC floss sets that mix to the photo’s colors, or only black or white thread for a photo with little color. The best few are retested at full size. Fewer threads, lines or nails win if within 1% of the best score. Nails stay at least 3.5 mm apart.

    Colors are DMC floss numbers; screen colors are approximate. Lengths add 10% for knots and winding.
