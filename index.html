About

I’m an Integration & Test Specialist at SpaceX in Redmond, Washington, working on bus and payload sub-assemblies for Starlink satellites: production support, test-failure triage, and process improvement.

I studied mechanical engineering at Milwaukee School of Engineering, where I worked as an undergraduate research assistant on controls hardware, built the electronics for an ASME design competition robot, and led a high-power rocket team for the Wisconsin Space Grant Consortium competition.

Before SpaceX I was a Design Engineer I at Premier PV, designing wire harness systems for utility-scale solar projects and building internal engineering tools. I like getting into the weeds: debugging, troubleshooting, and figuring out how things work.
