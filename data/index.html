Data

Dashboards built from public data. Each shows its latest figures, a few charts, its sources and how it is measured. The date on the right is the period its newest data refers to.

  • Housing

    The latest figures and charts from public housing data, with sources and how each figure is measured.

     

  • Economy

    The latest figures and charts from public economic data, with sources and how each figure is measured.

     

  • Prices vs. Inflation

    How prices in 32 categories have changed since a year you choose, next to overall inflation. Also the S&P 500 adjusted for the cost of essentials.

     

  • Markets

    Market figures from open sources and an event calendar with predictions registered before each event.

     

  • Predictions

    Forecasts made from the data we collect, the markets they affect, and the record of past hits and misses. Every model is backtested first.

     

  • Collection status

    When each data feed behind the dashboards last ran, how many rows it holds, and whether it is healthy.
