How it works

  1. Order and send a photo

    Choose a package and pay at checkout. Then send the photo you want to use.

  2. The guide is prepared

    The crop, contrast, and settings are adjusted by hand and the result is checked. You receive the guide within 3 days.

  3. Print and build

    Print the template at full size, put a nail at each mark, and wind the thread in the order listed.

Questions

Which photos work best?
A clear, well-lit photo of one face or a simple subject, with the subject filling most of the frame.
How long does it take?
You receive the guide within 3 days of sending your photo.
How do I send my photo?
Instructions for sending your photo appear after checkout.
What do I need to build it?
A board larger than the template, one small nail for each mark on the template, thread, a hammer, and tape to join the template pages.
Can I make one myself for free?
Yes. The String Art Maker on this site is free and stays free. It runs in your browser and makes the same kind of guide from your own settings.
