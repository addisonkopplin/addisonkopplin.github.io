Privacy Policy: Shorts Poster

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Shorts Poster is a personal tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel to upload videos, update video details, and read and reply to comments on that channel, using the YouTube Data API.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it accesses that owner's own YouTube channel data (videos, playlists, comments, and basic analytics) through Google OAuth.

How data is used

Data is used only to manage the owner's own channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own machine and are never published. No data from other users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Shorts Poster's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/addisonkopplin/science-shorts-sims/issues.
